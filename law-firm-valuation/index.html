Law firm valuations

What is your law firm really worth?

A realistic valuation is the starting point for any sale, merger or succession plan. We give UK law firm owners a clear, confidential view of what their practice is worth, and how to increase it.

Start a confidential conversation → All services

What drives the value of a law firm

No two firms are valued the same way, but buyers consistently look at the same things:

  • Fee income and profit, and how steady they have been
  • The mix of work, and how much of it recurs (for example wills and probate, conveyancing, or repeat commercial clients)
  • How loyal the clients are, and whether a few clients account for most of the fees
  • Whether key relationships sit with the owner or are shared across the team
  • Work in progress, debtors and how quickly bills are paid
  • Compliance record, complaints and indemnity claims history
  • Location, premises and the systems the firm runs on

How we approach a valuation

  1. Initial conversationWe discuss your firm, your plans and your timescale, in confidence.
  2. Review of your figuresWe look at your accounts, fee income by work type, work in progress and team structure.
  3. Valuation and explanationWe give you a realistic range and explain what is driving it, so you understand how buyers will see your firm.
  4. Ways to increase valueWe suggest practical steps to strengthen your position, whether you plan to sell next year or in five years.

Increasing the value of your practice

Most owners can improve their firm's value before a sale. Common steps include spreading client relationships across the team, building recurring work, tidying up work in progress and debtors, and making sure compliance records are in good order.

The earlier you start, the more difference these steps make.

Ask a question →
How much is my law firm worth?

It depends mainly on profit, the quality and mix of your fee income, your client base and your team. We give each firm an individual valuation rather than relying on a rule of thumb.

Is a valuation confidential?

Yes. Nobody outside our team knows you have asked, and your information is never shared without your agreement.

Do I have to sell if I get a valuation?

No. Many owners ask for a valuation to plan ahead, prepare for a partner leaving or understand their options.

What information do you need?

Usually your recent accounts, fee income by work type, details of work in progress and debtors, and an outline of your team. We will tell you exactly what is useful.
