Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to sell a law firm?

It varies with the size and type of practice and the market, but many sales take several months from first instruction to completion. We will give you a realistic timescale at the outset.

Will my staff and clients find out I am selling?

Not from us. Approaches to potential buyers are made on an anonymous basis, and your firm is only identified once you are happy to proceed with a specific party.

Do you provide law firm valuations?

Yes. We provide valuations based on your fee income, profitability, work types and client base, along with practical advice on improving value.

What size of firm do you work with?

We work with firms of all sizes, from sole practitioners through to multi-office practices.

Can you help if I am looking to buy or merge?

Yes. We work with firms looking to grow through acquisition or merger, and can help identify suitable opportunities.

Do you recruit for law firms?

Yes. Our Legal Recruitment division places solicitors, paralegals and training contract candidates with national UK law firms.
