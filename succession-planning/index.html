Succession planning

Plan your exit before it becomes urgent.

Every law firm owner will one day step back. A clear succession plan protects the value you have built, gives your team and clients certainty, and keeps your options open.

Start a confidential conversation → All services

Why plan early

Owners who leave succession too late often have fewer options and less negotiating power. Ill health, a partner leaving or a change in the market can force a decision at the worst time.

Starting a few years ahead gives you time to grow value, develop the next generation and choose the route that suits you best.

Your succession options

  • Internal succession: passing ownership to partners or senior staff
  • Sale to another law firm
  • Merger with a firm that can take on your clients and team
  • Bringing in a partner or investor over time
  • A gradual exit, with you staying on as a consultant

What a succession plan covers

We help you build a practical plan covering:

  • Your timescale and what you want from your exit
  • A realistic view of what the firm is worth today
  • Who will hold client relationships and key roles in future
  • Regulatory roles, including who will act as COLP and COFA
  • Indemnity insurance and, if the firm closes without a successor, run-off cover
  • How and when to tell your team and clients

When the time comes

When you are ready to act, we can help you carry out the plan, from finding a buyer or merger partner to completing the deal.

FAQ

Succession planning: your questions.

Anything else you'd like to know? Ask us in confidence.

Ask a question →
When should I start succession planning?

Ideally several years before you plan to step back. That gives you time to increase value and develop the people who will take over.

What happens if I close my firm without a successor?

You would usually need to buy run-off indemnity cover for past work, and arrange for client files to be stored and dealt with. A sale or merger can often avoid this.

Can my partners or staff buy the firm?

Yes. Internal succession is common, and we can help you work out a fair price and a structure the next owners can afford.

Is a succession conversation confidential?

Completely. Many owners speak to us years before they tell anyone else.
