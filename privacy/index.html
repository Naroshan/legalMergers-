Privacy policy

How we look after your information.

Confidentiality is at the heart of what we do. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, how long we keep it and the rights you have. Last updated 26 November 2026.

Who we are

Legal Mergers ("we", "us") is a trading style of The LeadGenCo LTD, a company registered in England & Wales (company no. 17274904), with its registered office at 30 St Mary Axe, London EC3A 8BF. We provide law firm sales, mergers, acquisitions, valuation and succession advice, and specialist legal recruitment, through this website at legalmergers.co.uk. The LeadGenCo LTD is the controller of the personal information described in this policy.

For any question about this policy or your information, email info@legalmergers.co.uk or write to Adam Reeve, Head of Legal, at adam@legalmergers.co.uk.

Information we collect

When you make an enquiry by email or through our contact form, we collect your name, firm, email address, phone number, what you are interested in and anything you tell us in your message. If we go on to work together, this may include information about your firm, its finances, staff and clients.

When you register as a candidate or send us your CV, we collect your name, contact details, preferred location and practice area, and the information in your CV, such as your qualifications, employment history and salary expectations. Please don't include sensitive information, such as health details or ethnicity, unless it is relevant to your application.

When a law firm instructs us to recruit, we collect the contact details of the people we deal with and details of the role.

When you visit the website, our hosting provider automatically records technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, for security and to keep the site running.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and provide the services you ask for
  • To find suitable buyers, sellers or merger partners, always without naming you or your firm until you agree
  • To match candidates with suitable roles and put them forward to law firms, only with their permission
  • To find suitable candidates for law firms that instruct us
  • To keep records, meet our legal and regulatory obligations and deal with any complaints
  • To keep this website secure and working properly

Our legal basis

Under UK data protection law we rely on:

  • Taking steps at your request before a contract, and performing a contract, when you ask us to act for you
  • Legitimate interests in running our business, responding to enquiries and introducing candidates and firms to each other, balanced against your rights
  • Consent before we send a candidate's CV to a law firm, which you can withdraw at any time
  • Legal obligation where the law requires us to keep or share information

Who we share it with

We never sell your information. We share it only when needed:

  • With prospective buyers, sellers or merger partners, only with your agreement and normally after they have signed a confidentiality agreement
  • With law firms about a specific role, only with the candidate's permission
  • With service providers who support us, such as email and website hosting, under contracts that protect your information
  • With professional advisers, regulators or authorities where the law requires it

International transfers

Some of our service providers, including our website host (GitHub) and Google, which supplies the fonts used on this site, may process information outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on safeguards recognised under UK law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or adequacy regulations.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't lead to work: up to 2 years from our last contact
  • Candidate details and CVs: up to 2 years from our last contact, unless you ask us to delete them sooner
  • Records of work we carry out for clients: up to 6 years after the matter ends, to meet legal and accounting requirements
  • Website server logs: kept by our hosting provider for a limited period for security

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of the information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete
  • Ask us to delete your information
  • Ask us to restrict how we use it, or object to how we use it
  • Ask us to transfer it to you or someone else in a usable format
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent

To use any of these rights, email info@legalmergers.co.uk. We will reply within one month. There is normally no charge.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not currently use analytics. If you close the "Call Adam Reeve" message, your browser remembers that choice until you close the tab, using storage that stays on your device and is not shared with us. If we add analytics in future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required.

Keeping your information secure

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information, and limit access to people who need it. Confidentiality agreements are used before any details of a firm or candidate are shared.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first at info@legalmergers.co.uk. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated at the top.
