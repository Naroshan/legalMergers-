Recruitment FAQs

Do you charge candidates a fee?

No. Our service is free for candidates. Our fees are paid by the hiring law firm.

Will you send my CV anywhere without asking?

Never. We always discuss a role with you and get your permission before putting you forward.

Can you help me find a training contract?

Yes. We work with candidates seeking training contracts and SQE qualifying work experience, and can advise on how to strengthen your applications.

Which locations do you cover?

We place candidates with national law firms across the UK, including hybrid and remote roles where available.
