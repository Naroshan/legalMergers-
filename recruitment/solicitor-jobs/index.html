Solicitor jobs

Solicitor jobs with national UK law firms.

We place qualified solicitors into roles with national law firms across England and Wales, in specialist practice areas. Law firms come to us for solicitors who are the right fit for their team, not just the right CV.

For candidates

For solicitors looking for a new role

Whether you want more responsibility, a better balance, a step up to partnership or a move into a new area, we help you find a firm where you will thrive. We talk to you first about what you want, then approach firms on your behalf.

Your search stays confidential. We never send your CV anywhere without your permission, and we never contact your current employer.

Roles we place

  • Associates and senior associates
  • Legal directors and partners
  • Team leaders and heads of department
  • Consultant solicitors
  • Hybrid and remote roles where available

For law firms

For law firms hiring solicitors

Hiring the wrong solicitor is expensive, and a good one can be hard to reach through job adverts alone. Many of the best candidates are not actively applying. We know the legal market and can approach them discreetly.

How we help

  • A clear brief: the work, the team, the client base and what success looks like
  • Targeted search across your practice area and location
  • Pre-screened shortlists, with experience and motivation checked
  • Interview arrangements, feedback and offer negotiation

FAQ

Solicitor jobs: your questions.

Anything else you'd like to know? Ask Adam in confidence.

Get in touch →
Do you charge solicitors a fee?

No. Our service is free for candidates. Our fees are paid by the hiring law firm.

Can I look for a role confidentially?

Yes. We never share your CV without your permission and never approach your current firm.

Which practice areas do you recruit for?

Most areas of private practice, including conveyancing, private client, family, commercial property, employment, litigation and personal injury.

How do law firms instruct you?

Send us details of the role, or ask Adam Reeve for a call to talk through what you need. We will agree a brief and start the search.
