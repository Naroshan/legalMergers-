FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you're looking for? We're happy to talk it through in confidence.

Ask us a question →
How long does it take to sell a law firm?

It depends on the size and type of practice and the market, but many sales take several months from instruction to completion. We'll give you a realistic timescale at the outset.

Will my staff and clients find out I'm selling?

Not from us. Approaches are made anonymously, and your firm is only identified once you're happy to proceed with a specific party.

Do you provide law firm valuations?

Yes. We value practices based on fee income, profitability, work types and client base, with practical advice on improving value.

What size of firm do you work with?

Firms of all sizes, from sole practitioners through to multi-office practices.

Can you help if I'm looking to buy or merge?

Yes. We work with firms looking to grow through acquisition or merger and can help identify suitable opportunities.

Do you recruit for law firms?

Yes. Our Legal Recruitment team places solicitors, paralegals and training contract candidates with national UK law firms.
